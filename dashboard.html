Goals & settings

Goals (active hours)

WHO and ILO link regular weeks of 55 hours or more to higher stroke and heart-disease risk. Tracked active time is lower than time at the desk, so set limits well below that line.

Work window

Activity outside this window, on other days, or after you close the day counts as after hours. Activity before the day start counts toward the previous day.
